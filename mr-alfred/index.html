Mr. Alfred

A private household assistant

Mr. Alfred is a personal assistant used only by one household. Family members talk to him by voice or text message, and he keeps the household's notes, calendar and reminders in order.

He is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and only members of the household can use him. He never acts on his own. Every calendar change and every email he sends is first shown to a household member and happens only after they approve it.
