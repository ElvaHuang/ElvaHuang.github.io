Privacy policy

Mr. Alfred · Last updated September 27, 2026

Mr. Alfred is a private assistant used only by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it uses

Mr. Alfred signs in to its own Google account and asks for two permissions:

  • Google Calendar events: to list, create and update events on the calendars the household has shared with it.
  • Gmail send: to send emails that a household member has approved. It cannot read anyone's inbox.

Every calendar change and email is shown to a household member first, and happens only after they approve it.

How data is handled

  • Data is used only to carry out the household's own requests.
  • Records of requests and approvals are kept on the household's own computer. Google sign-in credentials are stored there too, readable only by its owner.
  • To understand a request, its text (and relevant calendar details) is processed by the AI and speech-to-text services Mr. Alfred runs on. Nothing is used for advertising.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Mr. Alfred's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by un-sharing a calendar with Mr. Alfred.
